What does “AA pass” mean?

Normal text needs at least 4.5:1 contrast; large text needs 3:1. Large means at least 24 CSS px, or about 18.7 px when bold. Pass/fail uses the full ratio, before rounding.

These checks compare solid sRGB backgrounds with pure black or white text. They do not certify an entire design as accessible. Read the WCAG contrast guidance ↗
